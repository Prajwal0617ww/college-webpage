About Us

Our college was established in 1960 by Shri Shivaji Education Society, founded by Dr. Panjabrao Deshamukh with a mission to provide top-notch education. We offer a dynamic learning environment, modern facilities, and experienced faculty. It is affiliated with Sant Gadagebaba Amaravati University.

Courses Offered

  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc)
  • Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)
  • Bachelor of Arts (B.A)
  • Master of Science (M.Sc)
  • Master of Arts (M.A)
  • Master of Commerce (M.Com)
  • M.C.V.C. (Minimum Competency Vocational Courses)
